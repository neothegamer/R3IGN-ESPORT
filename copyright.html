Copyright Policy

R3IGN respects intellectual property rights and provides a process for reporting copyright concerns involving content on our platform.

Last updated: 09/09/2026.

01

Scope

This Copyright Policy explains how R3IGN (“R3IGN,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles copyrighted material on our platform, including our website, leagues, rankings, events, organizations, player market, highlights, and news sections (collectively, the “Service”). It applies to all users, organizations, and content submitted to or displayed on R3IGN.

02

Respect for Intellectual Property

R3IGN respects the intellectual property rights of others and expects users of the Service to do the same. We do not permit content that infringes the copyright, trademark, or other intellectual property rights of any person or entity.

This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Team logos, branding, or organization assets used without permission.
  • Music, artwork, or other media used without a valid license.
03

User-Submitted Content

By submitting content to R3IGN, including highlights, team or organization profiles, or player market listings, you represent that you own the content or have obtained all necessary rights, licenses, and permissions to submit and display it on R3IGN.

You retain ownership of content you submit, but grant R3IGN a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free license to host, display, and distribute that content as part of operating the Service.

04

Reporting Copyright Infringement

If you believe content on R3IGN infringes your copyright, submit a notice to r3ignhq@gmail.com including:

  1. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed.
  2. Identification and location of the allegedly infringing material.
  3. Your name, address, telephone number, and email address.
  4. A statement of good-faith belief that the use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  5. A statement, made under penalty of perjury, that the information is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on the owner’s behalf.
  6. Your physical or electronic signature.
05

Counter-Notification

If you believe content you submitted was removed or disabled in error, submit a counter-notice to r3ignhq@gmail.com including:

  1. Identification of the removed material and its location on the Service before removal.
  2. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that you have a good-faith belief the material was removed as a result of mistake or misidentification.
  3. Your name, address, telephone number, and email address, together with a statement consenting to the jurisdiction of the federal court in your district, or an applicable jurisdiction, and accepting service of process from the person who filed the original complaint.
  4. Your physical or electronic signature.

Upon receipt of a valid counter-notice, we may reinstate the removed content unless the original complainant informs us that they have filed a legal action seeking a court order to restrain the alleged infringement.

06

R3IGN reserves the right to suspend or terminate the accounts of users or organizations found to be repeat infringers of third-party intellectual property rights, at our sole discretion.
